## 📝 App Store Long Description

### THE HOUSE NEVER ENDS

INFINITY HOUSE is a pixel-art endless runner set in a surreal haunted mansion that defies reality. Every corridor leads deeper into madness. Every door hides secrets. Every run brings you closer to the truth.

### CORE GAMEPLAY

**Auto-Run Platforming**
- Tight jump physics with coyote time and buffering
- Navigate deadly hazards: spikes, goo, crumbling platforms
- Choose doors wisely: Safe, Reward, Mystery, or Locked

**Three Supernatural Pursuers**
- THE SHADOW: Relentless pursuer from the left
- THE CRAWLER: Ceiling ambusher with slow debuff
- THE MIMIC: Disguised as doors and objects

**Dynamic Chase Meter**
- Fills from mistakes and hesitation
- Monsters intensify as meter rises
- Freeze it with rare Chase Crystals

### FUSION SYSTEM

**5-to-1 Fusion Chamber**
Transform five items of the same rarity into one of the next tier. 100% success rate. Use Catalysts for 10% chance to skip a tier entirely.

**6 Rarity Tiers:**
- Common (Gray, 55%)
- Rare (Blue, 25%)
- Epic (Purple, 12%)
- Legendary (Gold, 6%)
- Mythic (Crimson with violet glow, 1.5%)
- **Exotic (Iridescent black, 0.1-0.2%)** - Trade-only on Steam!

**Condition System:**
Every skin drops with a unique Condition value (0.0-1.0):
- **Blessed** (0.0-0.25): Bright, pure, worth +15% on Steam Market
- **Awakened** (0.25-0.5): Clean and vibrant
- **Withered** (0.5-0.75): Dull and worn
- **Cursed** (0.75-1.0): Dark and flickering

Condition affects brightness, visual effects, and market value!

### CHESTS & VAULTS

**Basic Chest** - Free after runs (Common-Rare)
**Crystal Chest** - 500 Shards or 50 Diamonds (Rare-Epic)
**Mythic Chest** - 200 Diamonds (Epic-Legendary-Mythic)
**Eternal Vault** - Event-only (Legendary-Mythic-**Exotic**)
**Obsidian Vault** - 500 Diamonds (Guaranteed Legendary+, **Exotic** chance)

### DUAL CURRENCY ECONOMY

**💎 Diamonds (Premium)**
- Earned through events, milestones, or purchase
- Used for premium chests and exclusive cosmetics
- Cross-platform progression

**✦ Shards (Utility)**
- Earned from duplicates, fusion, and runs
- Used for Crystal Chests, Catalysts, and boosts
- Convert duplicates instantly (mobile only)

### STEAM MARKET INTEGRATION (PC ONLY)

**All skins are tradeable on Steam Community Market!**
- Buy and sell cosmetics with real money
- Exotics ONLY available on Steam Market
- Condition affects market price (Blessed skins = premium)
- No direct trades - all transactions through Steam

### SPECIAL ROOMS

**Safe Room** (Every 3 runs)
- Shop access
- Fusion Chamber terminal
- Rest before next corridor

**Reward Room**
- Choose from 3 chests (only one is real)
- Lore-based puzzles
- High-risk, high-reward

**Alchemy Room**
- Physical fusion altar with lore effects
- Cinematic transformation sequences
- Mysterious ambient soundscapes

### PROGRESSION & EVENTS

**Season Pass**
- Free and Premium tracks
- Exclusive cosmetics
- Daily and weekly quests
- 50 tiers of rewards

**Weekend Events**
- +50% Shard Bonus
- Limited-time Eternal Vaults
- Featured cosmetic rotations

**Perfect Run Bonuses**
- No damage = +200% Shards
- Guaranteed chest reward
- Prestige tracking

### PLATFORM FEATURES

**Cross-Platform (PC/Mac/iOS/Android)**
- Unified progression across all devices
- Platform-specific features:
  - PC/Mac: Steam Market, full fusion
  - Mobile: Gesture controls, duplicate→Shard conversion

**Fair Monetization**
- 100% cosmetic-only
- No pay-to-win
- All content earnable through gameplay
- Transparent odds disclosure

**Privacy-First**
- Analytics opt-in
- GDPR compliant
- No forced ads
- No tracking without consent
